# MVP Seed Lists

## Document Purpose

This document provides the minimum business seed lists for MVP categories. These
lists are starting points for planning and UX clarity, not final long-term taxonomies.

## Contribution Seed Categories

### Task-Oriented Contributions

- Household chore
- Personal responsibility completed
- Family support/help
- Planning or organizing effort

### Recognition-Oriented Contributions

- Emotional care
- Thoughtful gesture
- Family initiative

## Reward Seed Categories

### Child-Facing Rewards

- Leisure time
- Small treat
- Special activity
- Choice privilege

### Family Or Adult-Facing Rewards

- Shared activity
- Personal break or rest time
- Appreciation gesture
- Small household favor

## Seed-List Rules

- MVP should start with a short, explainable set rather than a comprehensive
  catalog.
- Seed lists are for consistency and onboarding clarity, not for heavy
  customization.
- If a new category is needed during planning, it should map back to one of the
  business meanings above unless product explicitly expands scope.
