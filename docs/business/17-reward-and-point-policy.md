# Reward & Point Policy

## Document Purpose

This document closes the remaining business policy gaps around point earning,
point spending, reward ownership, and reward boundaries.

## Point Policy

### Task Points

- Primary practical currency for household contribution in MVP
- Freely eligible for reward requests within household rules
- Earned when the related contribution reaches approved state

### Love Points

- Recognition-oriented currency for emotional care and thoughtful actions
- Present in MVP, but more constrained than Task Points
- Should not be the default currency for material or highly transactional rewards

## Love Points Constraint

Default MVP policy:

- Love Points may support symbolic, appreciation-oriented, or low-material
  rewards.
- Love Points should not be assumed to unlock any material reward by default.
- If a reward uses Love Points, it should feel relationally safe and consistent
  with recognition rather than bargaining.

## Reward Policy Boundary

### Generally Appropriate Reward Types For MVP

- small treats;
- privileges or leisure time;
- shared activities;
- child rewards with clear household meaning;
- appreciation-oriented gestures.

### Avoid Or Exclude In MVP

- rewards that imply emotional coercion;
- rewards that feel manipulative or humiliating;
- rewards that create strong financial obligation;
- rewards that blur consent or personal boundaries.

## Reward Decision Authority

- Every reward must have one explicit decision owner.
- The decision owner handles `accept`, `reject`, and `delay`.
- A request does not force fulfillment even if the requester has enough points.

## Point Spending Timing

- Points are earned when the contribution is approved.
- Reward-request points are reserved when the request is submitted.
- Reserved points become spent when the request is accepted.
- Reserved points are returned immediately when the request is rejected or
  cancelled.
- Delay keeps the reservation active until a later decision.

## Adjustment Rule

- Finalized points may be corrected only through an adult exception path with a
  visible reason.
- Adjustment is intended for fairness correction, not as a routine play pattern.

## Planning Guardrails

- Do not make Love Points behave like a fully interchangeable second currency in
  MVP.
- Do not make reward redemption fully automatic based only on balance.
- Do not assume all reward types are equally valid; use the policy boundary
  above as the business filter.
