# Product Overview

## Product Goal

Family Operating System helps families make everyday contributions visible,
recognize emotional care, and exchange rewards in a respectful, game-like way.

## Problems To Solve

- Household work often feels invisible.
- One person can feel they carry more than others.
- Recognition is inconsistent, especially for emotional support.
- Children need clearer motivation and feedback loops.

## Primary Users

### Adult members

- Want fairness without turning the home into a workplace.
- Need a way to ask, recognize, and negotiate support more clearly.

### Managed child profiles

- Need simple motivation and clear feedback.
- Participate through adults instead of standalone access in MVP.

## Product Principles

1. Fairness: data should support conversation, not judgment.
2. Recognition: visible acknowledgment matters as much as task completion.
3. Voluntary exchange: reward requests can be accepted, rejected, or delayed.
4. Playful tone: the product should feel light, not supervisory.

## MVP Scope

### In Scope

- Create a family.
- Add adult members and managed child profiles.
- Record contributions.
- Approve or reject contributions when needed.
- Track Task Points and Love Points.
- Create rewards.
- Submit and decide reward requests.
- Show activity history.

### Out Of Scope

- Standalone child login.
- Leaderboards or winner/loser framing.
- Complex custom rules engine.
- Advanced gamification such as streaks, levels, or achievements.

## Core Product Loop

1. A member records a contribution.
2. The contribution is approved when policy requires it.
3. Points become visible.
4. A member uses points to request a reward.
5. The decision owner accepts, rejects, or delays that request.

## MVP Success Signals

- A family can finish the full loop end to end.
- Adults feel contributions are more visible.
- Reward exchanges stay voluntary and low-conflict.
- Child-facing views remain safe and simple.

## Near-Term Release Focus

After MVP works reliably, the first release should harden onboarding, activity
clarity, approval flows, and operational basics such as logging, error handling,
and deployment repeatability.
